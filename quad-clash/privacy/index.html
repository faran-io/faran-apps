Quad Clash support

Privacy policy

Quad Clash. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

Quad Clash is a game made by Faran, LLC.

No accounts, and play counts that never say who you are. Quad Clash has no accounts of its own, no chat and no way to share anything with other players. To learn where players stop playing, the game counts a few things on your device: how many games were started, finished or left early, how many people played each one, how far a tournament got, and steps such as the first launch. Once a day, at most, it sends us its totals since the last time, with only the date, the app version and whether it's the Android, iPhone or web version, and then deletes them from the device. The totals carry no ID, name, age answer or anything else that could tell your device apart from any other, and they're the same for every player of every age. We add them into daily totals for all players and keep nothing else: we don't keep your IP address, your device's details or the report itself. (Like any website, our host, Cloudflare, handles the connection on its servers under its own privacy policy.) They're never used for ads or to personalize anything. We don't run any other analytics or tracking of our own, and we never see who you are.

Your progress stays on your device. Your crew orbs, unlocked characters, best scores, settings, the answer to the age question and whether you bought Remove Ads are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them.

The age question. The first time the game starts, it asks how old the player is. The answer decides which ads may be shown and whether Google Play Games is used (see below), and stays on your device.

Google Play Games (Android). Players who answered the age question with 13 or over and have a Google Play Games profile are signed in to Play Games when the game opens, and the achievements earned on the device are recorded there. Google keeps them with that profile under its own privacy policy; we never see who you are. You can turn off Play Games sign-in, or delete what it keeps, in the Play Games app or your Google account.

Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between games, never during play and never when the game starts. Players 13 and over can also choose to watch an ad for bonus orbs. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, players 13 and over are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and can change their choice with PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's settings (the gear on the title screen).

Children. Quad Clash is made for kids and grown-ups alike. Before the age question is answered, no ads are loaded at all. For players under 13, every ad request is marked as directed to children: the advertising ID is not sent, ads are never personalized or based on interests, there is no remarketing, and only ads rated for general audiences are shown. Children never see the "watch an ad" offers, and changing the age answer sits behind a grown-ups' question. Players under 13 never use Google Play Games in Quad Clash.

Purchases. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. Google or Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns that the purchase was made, so it can stop showing ads.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
