Tie balloons to farm animals, float them home and pop your rivals' balloons.

Something wrong, or an idea? Email info@faran.io. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Tap PLAY for a round of Balloon Bandits. Walk up to something on the farm and your balloons tie on by themselves; once it has enough, it floats up to your crew's sky island. Your blaster fires on its own when you aim at balloons, and you tap to jump. CLASSIC is the island adventure with chests, bots and a boss.

Can we play together?

Yes. Phones on the same Wi-Fi find each other's rounds when you tap PLAY. To play with people over the internet, turn on ONLINE on the title screen. There are no names, no chat and no messages, online or on Wi-Fi.

Why does it ask my age?

So that players under 13 only ever get ads that are safe for children: not personalized, rated for general audiences, with no advertising ID sent. The answer stays on your device.

What are look packs?

Sets of looks (a suit, a hat, a backpack, a dance and a blaster wrap) in the Locker, $1.99 each. They never make you stronger. Parents can require approval for purchases in their Google Play or App Store family settings. If you reinstall or get a new phone, use Restore purchases in the same place.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own Google or iCloud backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
