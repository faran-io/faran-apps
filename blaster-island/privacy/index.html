Blaster Island support

Privacy policy

Blaster Island. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

Blaster Island is a game made by Faran, LLC.

No accounts, no chat, nothing that says who you are. Blaster Island has no sign-in, no chat, no player names and no way to send messages or share anything with other players. We never see who you are, and we don't track you.

Your progress stays on your device. Your level, coins, unlocked looks, look packs, settings and the answer to the age question are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

The age question. The first time the game starts, it asks how old the player is. The answer only decides which ads may be shown (see below) and stays on your device.

Anonymous counts. To see whether the game is fun and runs well, the app sends us counts of how it's played: when it's opened, how long it stays open and how many rounds that was, how each round or run ends and how long it took, how it was played (alone, on the same Wi-Fi or online) and with how many players, how smoothly it ran, and the first time a few things happen (such as finishing a first round or opening the locker). Each count also carries the app version and build, the day, and the day the game was first played on that phone, so we can see how many players come back. Nothing in it identifies the phone or the player: no name, no device or advertising ID, no age answer and no location. The counts go to our server, bandits.faran.io, which runs on Cloudflare, whether or not ONLINE is on. It sees your device's network (IP) address only to hold back floods of requests for a minute, and never stores it. It adds the counts straight into daily totals and keeps nothing else about them, apart from a random number sent with each batch of counts, used only so a batch sent twice isn't counted twice and deleted after about five weeks. Like any database, ours keeps backups of those totals for 30 days.

Playing together. Phones on the same Wi-Fi can find each other and play a round together. Playing over the internet is off until ONLINE is turned on in the game. Then the game connects to our game server, bandits.faran.io, which runs on Cloudflare. Either way, the game only sends what a round needs: where each player moves, their darts and balloons, and the look of their alien. It sends no names, messages or IDs. Like any internet service, the server sees your device's network (IP) address while you're connected; we don't store it or anything else about players, and a round is forgotten when it ends. The server keeps only daily totals, such as how many players joined and how many rounds were played. Cloudflare handles the connection as our host, under its privacy policy.

Ads. The game shows ads from Google AdMob between some rounds, never during play and never when the game starts. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, players 13 and over are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and can change their choice with AD CHOICES on the title screen.

Children. Blaster Island is made for kids and grown-ups alike. No ads are shown before the age question is answered. For players under 13, every ad request is marked as directed to children: the advertising ID is not sent, ads are never personalized or based on interests, there is no remarketing, and only ads rated for general audiences are shown. Parents can require approval for purchases in their Google Play family settings.

Purchases. Look packs are one-time purchases made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns which packs were bought, so it can unlock them.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
