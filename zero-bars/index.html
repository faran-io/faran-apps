How do I play a game?

Each game shows how to play the first time you open it. To see it again, tap the ⋯ button at the top right of the game, then How to play.

What's free, and what does the purchase add?

Eight games are free: Solitaire, Hearts, Sudoku, Block Puzzle, Checkers, Four in a Row, Snake and Bricks. One purchase unlocks all 31 games for good. There are no ads and no subscription.

I bought all the games. How do I get them on a new phone?

Sign in to the store with the same account, tap any game with a lock, then Restore purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device: your settings, favorites, best scores and any game you left unfinished, which opens where you left it. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does it need the internet?

No. Zero Bars plays fully offline, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Only buying or restoring the purchase talks to the store.
