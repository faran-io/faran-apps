Privacy policy

Zero Bars: Offline Games. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Zero Bars is a games app made by Faran, LLC.

We collect no data. Zero Bars has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app itself makes no network requests: every game plays fully offline.

Purchases. Unlocking all the games is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play or Apple's App Store, which handle payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The unlock is kept on your device, and Restore purchase asks the store for it again. On Android, Google's billing library, which the app uses to sell through Google Play, also sends Google its own diagnostic data.

Your progress stays on your device. Settings, favorites, best scores and games in progress are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children. Zero Bars collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
