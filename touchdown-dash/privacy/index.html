Touchdown Dash support

Privacy policy

Touchdown Dash. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

Touchdown Dash is a game made by Faran, LLC, for players 13 and over.

We receive nothing ourselves. Faran runs no servers for Touchdown Dash and never receives data about you or how you play. The game itself plays offline.

Ads. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob on Android and iPhone: one after some finished games, and an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra play card. To show and measure them, Google's ads code collects your device's advertising ID (Android) or other device identifiers (iPhone), your approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with the ads and diagnostic data, under Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The ads are rated for general audiences. On iPhone the game never asks to track you, so Google doesn't get your iPhone's advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first, and PRIVACY in the pause menu changes your choice. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Remove Ads. A one-time purchase through Google Play or the App Store ends the ads after games. Google or Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and we never see your payment details. The purchase is kept on your device, and the store restores it on a new phone signed in to the same Google account or Apple Account (RESTORE in the pause menu).

Your progress stays on your device. Your season, play cards, best scores, settings, Daily Drive results and the counts behind your achievements are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them.

Google Play Games (Android). If you have a Google Play Games profile, Play Games signs you in when the game opens, and the game sends Google the achievements you earn so they show on your profile. Google keeps them with your Play Games player ID, under its privacy policy. We don't receive your profile, your player ID or your achievements. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account. The game plays the same without it.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
