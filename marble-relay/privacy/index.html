Privacy policy

Marble Relay. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

Marble Relay is a game made by Faran, LLC.

No accounts, no analytics of our own. Marble Relay has no sign-in and no chat. We don't run analytics or tracking of our own, and we never see who you are.

Ads. Marble Relay shows ads from Google AdMob: now and then an ad between levels, from level 10 on and never during play, and, only when you choose it, a short video ad in exchange for a helper such as a hint or an extra undo. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

Purchases. The optional No Ads purchase goes through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only learns that the purchase was made, so it can stop the ads between levels. The video ads you can choose for helpers stay available. On Android, Google's billing library, which the game uses to sell through Google Play, also sends Google its own diagnostic data.

Your progress stays on your device. Levels, stars, coins and settings are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. Your device's own backup (iCloud on an iPhone or iPad, your Google account on Android) may include them, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them. Settings > Restore brings No Ads back after a reinstall or on a new device.

Children. Marble Relay is meant for players 13 and over. Its ads are limited to ones rated for general audiences.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listings before that version ships.
