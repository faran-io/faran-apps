Privacy policy

Letter Ride. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Letter Ride is a word game made by Faran, LLC.

We collect no data. Letter Ride has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app itself makes no network requests: every ride plays fully offline.

Your progress stays on your device. Your rides, stars, best scores, ride tickets, records, the words you've made, your Daily Ride streak, ride photos and settings are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing a ride. Share hands the ride photo and a line about the ride to your phone's share sheet, and the app you pick sends it. Nothing leaves the app unless you tap Share and choose where it goes.

Children. Letter Ride collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
