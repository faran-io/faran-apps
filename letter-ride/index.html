Something wrong, or an idea? Email info@faran.io. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device: your rides, records, Daily Ride streak and ride photos. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does it need the internet?

No. Letter Ride plays fully offline.
