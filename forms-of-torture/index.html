How do I play?

Every field of the form is its own little game with a goal, a timer and a few strikes: type a name on a keyboard of popping keys, stop an arrivals board on your birthday, dial a PIN that reshuffles, catch the Submit button. Win a field and the next one opens. Stars come from how fast you were or how few strikes you took.

It's too hard (or too easy)

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the cover. Each keeps its own stars and best times. After a few failed tries at a field, you can skip it.

Is it really just one page?

No.

What's Today's form?

One short form a day, the same for everyone: five fields and a boss, one twist, three strikes and one try. Finish it to keep your streak, then share your squares.

What's Resubmit?

An endless run of forms that get harder, opened by finishing page 1. Between forms, Gary updates the Terms: pick one of three cards. Curses move your ticket number faster; helpers don't. Three strikes and your application is rejected.

Ads and Remove Ads

The game is free. In the Android and iPhone apps an ad plays now and then between fields, never during one and never in Today's form. After a Resubmit rejection you can choose to watch one to appeal for one more strike. The No ads button on the cover is a one-time purchase that ends the ads between fields. It comes back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account, and on iPhone and iPad through Restore purchases on the cover.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device, except through your device's own Google or iCloud backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does it need the internet?

No, the game plays offline. Ads need a connection; without one the game just goes on.
